Executive Business Insights
Key findings from 100,400 daily stock records · Stock Market Data Warehouse · 2021–2026
1
NVDA dominates all-time returns at +1,253% — NVIDIA's 5-year price appreciation far exceeds every other stock in the warehouse. The next closest competitors (AMD +499%, GE +346%) trail by more than 2×, making NVDA the single most impactful holding in the Technology sector.
2
Technology delivers 9× the daily return of defensive sectors. Technology averages +0.0450%/day vs. Utilities at just +0.0048%/day. Over a 5-year compounding horizon this gap is the primary driver of portfolio outperformance.
3
Mega-cap stocks offer the best risk-adjusted returns. Sharpe proxy: Mega-cap at 0.0241 vs. Large-cap (0.0199) and Mid-cap (0.0180). Larger companies absorb volatility more effectively, providing superior returns per unit of risk across all 5 years analyzed.
4
2022 was a universal drawdown — 2023 was the strongest recovery year. Every sector posted negative daily returns in 2022 (Technology hit −0.0120%/day). The 2023 rebound was equally broad, with Technology reaching +0.0610%/day — the highest single-sector-year in the dataset.
5
Recommendation: A portfolio weighted toward Mega-cap Technology (30%), Communication Services (20%), and Financial Services (20%) with a defensive Utilities hedge (10%) would have delivered the highest risk-adjusted return over 2021–2026 based on this warehouse’s data. Rebalancing after the 2022 drawdown into Technology would have captured the maximum 2023 recovery.
